// 01 • Genesis & Student Initiative

Who We Are & Executive Summary

◈ ❈ ◈

ARVIX NEXUS 2026 is an inspiring student-driven movement initiated by students of Andhra University College of Engineering (AUCE) to foster grassroots innovation and technology leadership.

Host Institution

AUCE Student Initiative

Organized as a dedicated student-led initiative from Andhra University College of Engineering (AUCE), Visakhapatnam to empower young builders and foster national-level innovation.

AU North Campus

Hosted at AU North Campus, Andhra University, Visakhapatnam as our official hackathon venue, providing cutting-edge infrastructure and collaborative workspaces.

// 05 • 24-Hour Hackathon Timeline

24-Hour Event Agenda

◈ 🪔 ◈

Experience 24 hours of innovation, collaboration, and problem-solving at AU North Campus.

DAY 01

Kickoff, Ideation & Development Sprint

09:00 AM — Participant Registration & Check-in

Arrival, verification desk, WiFi access setup, and team kit allocation.

10:00 AM — Opening Ceremony & Hackathon Briefing

Welcome address, guidelines, judging parameters, and conclave inauguration.

11:00 AM — Problem Statement Release

Official release of challenge problem statements across all hackathon tracks.

11:30 AM — Team Formation & Ideation

Brainstorming, architectural design, scope defining, and roadmap finalization.

01:00 PM — Lunch Break

Networking and refreshments provided for all candidates.

02:00 PM — Development Begins

Official hacking begins. Intensive prototyping, coding, and engineering.

06:00 PM — Progress Review Checkpoint

Mentor review, checkpoint guidance, and intermediate technical evaluations.

09:00 PM — Final Development Sprint

Night development sprint, feature lockdown, and deployment testing.

DAY 02

Submissions, Demonstrations & Awards

09:00 AM — Project Submission Deadline

Code freeze, repository submission, and presentation upload deadline.

10:00 AM — Project Demonstrations & Judging

Live candidate demonstrations and Q&A sessions before the evaluation jury.

10:30 AM — Results Announcement, Prize Distribution & Closing Ceremony

Winner felicitations, cash prize awards, certificate distribution, and closing remarks.

Note: All activities are scheduled in Indian Standard Time (IST) at AU North Campus, Visakhapatnam.

// 06 • Transparent Evaluation

How Projects Will Be Judged

◈ ❈ ◈

Strictly weighted criteria ensures fairness, technical depth, and real-world applicability.

20%

Innovation & Originality

Uniqueness of the idea, creative problem-solving approach, and novelty compared to existing solutions.

20%

Real-World Impact

Relevance to societal or industrial problems, practical utility, and tangible benefit to end users.

20%

Technical Implementation

Code quality, architectural soundness, effective use of technologies, APIs, and functional completeness.

15%

Problem Understanding

Clarity in defining the root challenge, user persona analysis, and domain constraints awareness.

15%

Scalability & Feasibility

Viability for deployment at scale, cost feasibility, security considerations, and potential roadmap.

10%

Demonstration & Presentation

Effectiveness of the live prototype demo, articulate pitch, clear visual storytelling, and Q&A handling.
